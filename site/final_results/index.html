641/795 full-coverage predictions were correct. The selective policy accepted 574 images (541 correct, 33 errors), deferring 221. Accepted error was 5.75%, above the 1.5% target. Fusion weight 1.0 gives identical DNN and fusion predictions; no BN accuracy gain was demonstrated.

Grouped 95% CI: overall accuracy 77.90–83.48%; accepted accuracy 92.09–96.25%. Macro-F1 0.5502 uses all 156 class slots; test contains 135 species. Auto-resolved is a disagreement tag, not evidence of a DNN correction. No deferred case is credited as human-corrected.
